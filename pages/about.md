# About

Hey there. I'm Omer.

I've been coding since 4th grade, when my dad taught me BASIC and I got hooked. Since then I've written code in more languages than I can count, and it's still the part of my day I enjoy most.

My passion for application security started by accident, when I was offered the role of security champion. That journey taught me a lot, and it's still going. [OWASP](https://owasp.org) helped me along the way, so I became a paying member and led OWASP Glue. I spent years in DevSecOps, helping teams ship more secure software, including at Soluto and Snyk. I'm also a heavy open-source contributor, mostly to Kamus, a secret encryption solution for Kubernetes.

Along the way I gave a lot of talks, and wrote a lot about Kubernetes, CI/CD, and security. You can find those in the [archive](../archive/) and on the [talks page](../talks/).

Since 2022 I've been at [Ledge](https://ledge.co), where AI agents prepare the month-end close for finance teams. Learning what a real close looks like changed how I think about money, and I ended up running my own household the same way. That story is [my latest post](../posts/close-at-home/).

When I'm not working, I'm with my kids, or conquering the world in a strategy game. Budget-approved.
